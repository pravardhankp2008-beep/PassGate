Attendee Terms & Privacy Policy

Byte Race 2026 · VCET Puttur · PassGate Verification

1. Scope & Eligibility

This policy applies to all participants, hackathon teams, competitors, and attendees registering for admission passes to Byte Race 2026 at VCET Puttur. Registration constitutes a binding agreement to abide by event admissions protocols and campus safety guidelines.

2. Information We Collect & Purpose

We collect your Full Name, Email Address, and Ticket Tier (General, VIP, or Student Competitor) solely for the following legitimate operational purposes:

  • Generating a tamper-resistant, cryptographically formatted single-use QR admission pass.
  • Executing instantaneous, sub-second contactless gate check-in validation upon arrival.
  • Enforcing physical auditorium capacity thresholds in compliance with VCET Puttur venue safety guidelines.

3. Zero Commercial Monetization

PassGate maintains a strict zero-leakage privacy standard. Your personal data is never sold, rented, monetized, or shared with commercial advertisers, sponsors, or third-party marketing brokers. Data remains stored securely on encrypted infrastructure strictly for event management.

4. Single-Use Pass & Duplicate Lockout

Each QR ticket generated is strictly personal, non-transferable, and single-use. Upon first scan at the gate terminal, the ticket is atomically marked as redeemed. Any subsequent presentation of the same QR code will immediately trigger an “ENTRY DENIED: DUPLICATE” alert and entrance lockout.

5. Data Retention & Post-Event Purging

Attendee personal information is retained strictly for the duration of the hackathon event cycle. Within 30 days of the conclusion of Byte Race 2026, raw attendee PII is permanently purged, leaving only anonymized audit statistics.

6. Participant Code of Conduct

Attendees agree to conduct themselves professionally on VCET Puttur campus premises, honor hackathon arena regulations, and present valid physical identity verification upon request by gate stewards.
